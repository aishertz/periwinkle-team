Team Lore

The Periwinkle Guild

Beneath roofs of pink mushroom caps and lanterns hung from old timber beams, the Periwinkle team began as a quiet settlement of builders, gatherers, dreamers, and mildly suspicious resource hoarders.

Their banner carries magenta and purple, colors of twilight skies and enchanted forests. Their homes rise from moss, stone, oak, and fungal bloom, shaped by medieval craft and cottagecore softness.

They are not merely a team. They are a guild of cozy chaos, loyal hands, and blocks placed with dangerous levels of commitment.

Periwinkle carries a fairytale charm inspired by soft colors, woodland details, and the quiet magic of fairy dust. In the server, the team is also called Winkle, a shorter name that keeps the same playful spirit while sounding like something whispered between lanterns and leaves.

Their build style is flexible, shifting between medieval, modern, fantasy, steampunk, and cottagecore themes while still keeping the team’s signature enchanted softness.
